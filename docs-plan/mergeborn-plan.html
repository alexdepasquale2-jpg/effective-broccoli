Mergeborn

A card battler where you tap, summon and merge cards that fight on their own. Like Revolution Idle, every new layer changes how the earlier ones work, so the grind keeps turning into a new game.

Resets, Revolution Idle style

Five layers, each automating the one below

Every layer resets the ones beneath it, pays a new currency, and changes a rule. Once a layer is automated, its old grind runs in the background.

LAYERL1

Shuffle

unlocks Stage 25 bossresets Gold, board, stagepays Essence

Essence shop: starting tier, summon odds, tap multiplier. Unlocks Tap stage 2.

LAYERL2

Ascend

unlocks 10 Shuffles or 1e4 Essenceresets + Essence shoppays Sigils

A new element joins the pool: Fire → Water → Earth → Air → Light → Void. Each one adds a counter mechanic.

LAYERL3

Codex

unlocks every card up to T12resets collection progresspays Pages

Permanent card skills: on-merge, on-death, aura. Unlocks Merge stage 3.

LAYERL4

Era

unlocks each Era's final bossresets everything belowpays Era Shards

The rules mutate. See the five Eras below.

LAYERL5

Revolution

unlocks Era 5resets allpays Revolutions

Automation for every lower layer, plus challenge runs (no tap, no merge, 3×3 board) with permanent multipliers.

Layer 4 rule mutators

Each Era plays like a new game

I

Base rules

Tap, summon, merge and battle as learned.

II

Decay

Cards lose power over time. Merge often or fall behind.

III

Hex board

Six neighbours per slot. Synergies get richer.

IV

Enemies merge

Foes fuse mid-fight. Kill pairs before they combine.

V

Turncoat

Summon enemy cards onto your board.

Pacing target

Each Shuffle runs faster than the last

Shuffle #1 takes about 8 minutes, and #10 takes about 90 seconds. Each reset should feel like a power spike, not a setback.

Keeping the grind fun

Hooks

  • Variable rewards. Summon odds, lucky +2 merges, crit chains. Pity timers cap bad luck.
  • Goal ladder. The next three unlocks are always on screen with progress bars.
  • Juicy merges. Screen shake, rising sound pitch per tier, new art at T5, T10, T15.
  • Unlock cadence. New unlock every 30–90 s early, every 5–10 min mid-game, every reset late.
  • Offline progress. Up to 8 h (raised by upgrades), with a "while you were away" summary.
  • Fair play. No paid currency, no countdown sales, no loot boxes for money.
