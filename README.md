# Let It Pass · Action Meets Stillness

An action game where doing nothing is your greatest power.

## 🏃 Action Meets Inaction

Move freely through a mystical realm where crises, storms, and human moments emerge dynamically in 2D space:
- 🌪️ **Loud Crises (Dust Devils, Brush Fires, Runaway Wagons, Tavern Brawls):** Appear violently with spinning debris and fiery particles. If you stay calm or step back, they naturally blow themselves out. Over-reacting or striking causes chaos.
- 💧 **Quiet Moments (Weeping Strangers, Wilted Saplings):** Subtle and fragile. Standing peacefully beside them inside their aura for a few seconds **tends** to their pain and heals them.
- 💥 **Direct Strikes (Space / Strike Button):** Releases a kinetic force shockwave for the rare crises (Trapped Lamb, Fallen Elder) that genuinely require your physical strength.
- 🧘 **Stillness Aura:** Standing still expands your peaceful aura radius and accelerates your **Stillness Score & Multiplier** (up to ×3.0).

## Controls

- **Movement:** `WASD`, Arrow Keys, or on-screen Touch Virtual Joystick.
- **Strike / Action:** `Space`, `J`, or the **💥 Strike** button.
- **Tend / Be Present:** Walk into the radius of a quiet event and stand completely still for 2 seconds.
