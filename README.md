# Crop Survivors · Merge Farming & Ally Summoning

A top-down arcade farming and horde-survival roguelite fusion!

## 🌟 Merge-Farming & Ally Summoning

- 🌱 **Merge Crop Mechanics:** Drag identical crops together on your farm plots to merge them:
  - **Level 1 (Standard)** + **Level 1** ➔ **Level 2 (Large ⭐)**
  - **Level 2** + **Level 2** ➔ **Level 3 (Giant 🌟)**
- 🧙‍♂️ **Harvest to Summon Powerful Allies:**
  - **Parsnip (Giant)** ➔ **Root Sprite** (Swift woodland fairy shooting piercing thorn arrows)
  - **Potato (Giant)** ➔ **Earthen Spud Golem** (Heavy tank slamming the ground with AOE shockwaves)
  - **Strawberry (Giant)** ➔ **Berry Bloodstalker** (Lifestealing phantom that heals your health)
  - **Cauliflower (Giant)** ➔ **Ironwood Treant** (Colossus summoning wide clusters of earthquake root spikes)
  - **Starfruit (Giant)** ➔ **Astral Wyrm** (Cosmic dragon breathing searing plasma beams across the horde)

## 📜 Persistent Farmstead Legacy

Reinvest gold earned across seasons into Land Deed Expansions, Greenhouse Engines, Starting Wealth, and Blessed Soil!

## Controls

- **Touch / Mobile:** Drag anywhere for virtual dynamic joystick. Drag matching crops onto each other to merge. Tap ✨ Auto Merge to merge all matching crops. Walk onto plots to plant/water/harvest. Tap 🏡 Shop or walk up to Farmhouse.
- **Keyboard / Desktop:** `WASD` or Arrow Keys to move. `Space` or `E` to interact. `M` to Auto-Merge. `B` for Farm Shop.
