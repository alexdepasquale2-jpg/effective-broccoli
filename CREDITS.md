# Credits

## Tiny Speck and _Glitch_

Project Glimmer exists because of [Tiny Speck](https://www.tinyspeck.com/), who in 2013–2014 did
something almost unheard of: after closing _Glitch_, they released the game's art, animation, music,
and server-side game code into the **public domain** under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

That release included roughly 10,000 art and animation assets and the complete server-side JavaScript
that defined every item, skill, quest, recipe, and achievement in the game. Project Glimmer is built
directly on that gift.

CC0 waives all copyright and imposes no attribution requirement. We credit Tiny Speck anyway, because
it is the right thing to do.
