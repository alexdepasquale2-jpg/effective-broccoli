A year in a valley · a game about not doing things

Let It Pass

Twelve days. Five corners of a life — yourself, your kin, your work, the town, the world. Stories run through all of them whether you are in them or not.

Let it pass — do nothing. Free. Usually right.

Watch — spend a little to see what a moment actually needs.

Tend — be present without fixing. Costs you.

Act — put your hand in. Costs you a great deal.

Loud things are rarely the ones that need you — but not never, so you cannot make it a rule. Every intervention tires you, and each one in a day costs more than the last. Run empty and you lose the day.

At the end you will be shown each story's ending beside the ending it would have had if you had never touched it.
