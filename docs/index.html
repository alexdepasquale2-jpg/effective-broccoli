A game about not doing things

Let It Pass

Things will arrive. Each one offers you something to do about it.

You can act. You can also sit there. Most of what arrives resolves the same either way — but acting always costs you something, and the loud things are almost never the ones that need you.

Stillness accrues while you leave the world alone. At the end, the ledger will tell you how many of your interventions changed anything at all.
